What this is not, and known limits
  • Not a trading bot and never an order: read-only market data, no Trade permission, no Agentic Account. The trader decides.
  • Exchange holidays are not modelled: on a US holiday the session flag reads "open" during regular hours.
  • bitget-signal is additive. When its upstreams are down, rows report error and coverage caps confidence — the desk does not fill the gap with a guess.
  • The judge's verdict is lint-clean (no buy/sell/long/short). The analysts' transcript is quoted argument and may echo the words in your thesis.
  • Every number on the card is computed by code; the model interprets and argues. Rows marked argued are a model's web-sourced brief.
